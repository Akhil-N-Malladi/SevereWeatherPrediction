Examples of alerts that aligned with later severe-weather reports.

These sample cases show how the system can be summarized for an oversight audience: location, hazard, signal, and the public-safety implication.

Extreme Heat

The model correctly predicted an extreme heat event in Frisco, Texas on March 17, 2026, flagging a higher-than-normal afternoon heat index before local observations confirmed the risk.

Heavy Rain

A heavy-rain signal was elevated across central Arkansas, giving staff a concise flood-readiness cue for vulnerable transportation corridors and low-lying neighborhoods.
